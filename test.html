RichGophishClient

RichGophishClient is a multiplatform application I created to streamline managing phishing campaigns for different clients. The app consolidates all GoPhish instances in one place (multi-tenancy), uses AI to automatically generate realistic phishing emails and pages, and creates advanced reports with charts.

Where This Project Came From

While working at a cybersecurity company, I was tasked with conducting phishing campaigns for various clients. I quickly realized how cumbersome it was to manage multiple GoPhish instances - each client required separate login, separate configuration, separate reports.

Additionally, creating convincing phishing emails for each campaign took enormous amounts of time. I had to constantly come up with new scenarios, adapt the tone to specific companies, pay attention to details that would make the message seem authentic.

I thought to myself: "There has to be a better way to do this." And I decided to create a tool that would make my life easier.

What I Built

I designed a multiplatform application that solves three key problems:

AI-powered content creation - I integrated language models that automatically generate realistic phishing emails and pages. Just describe the scenario, and AI prepares several variants tailored to a specific company.

Multi-tenancy - One application manages all GoPhish instances. Instead of jumping between different systems, I have one central hub for handling all clients.

Advanced reporting - Automatic generation of charts and summaries that allow quick analysis of results and comparison of campaign effectiveness between different clients.

Why AI Makes Sense Here

Creating a good phishing email is an art. It must be convincing, but not too obvious. It must mimic a specific company's style, use appropriate language, contain realistic details.

AI allows me to generate dozens of variants in minutes instead of hours. I can quickly test different approaches, different scenarios, different levels of "suspiciousness." This means more time for analyzing results and less time on boring content preparation.

How This Changed My Work

Instead of spending half my time on administrative tasks, I can focus on what really matters - analyzing vulnerabilities and advising clients on how to improve security.

High-quality email and page templates allowed me to reach a higher level of phishing tests. LLMs, without moderation, handle this task well, resulting in fast content delivery and high test diversification.

Value for Others

What I initially created for myself turned out to be a solution to a problem many specialists in the industry face. Anyone working with multiple clients in cybersecurity can benefit from this type of tool.

AI in cybersecurity isn't just about defending against threats - it's also intelligent support for people conducting these tests.
