Websites & Web Apps

Need a company website, an online store, or a blog? I’ll build and launch it around what you need it to do: bring in enquiries, sell products, or let you publish on your own. If an off-the-shelf tool doesn’t fit, I’ll build a custom web app, from a customer portal to an internal tool that connects to the systems your team already uses.

Native Mobile & Desktop Apps

I’ll build a native app for the platform your users work on. I’ll focus on fast startup, a responsive interface, and efficient use of system resources. I’ll use built-in platform features and hardware capabilities where they make the app faster or easier to use.

AI Integration & Automation

Give AI a task, not just a question. I’ll automate the parts of your workflow where your team still copies data, routes requests, or chases the next step. An assistant can read an incoming message, check the CRM, create a task, and notify the right person. When a decision is needed, it can get everything ready for review and wait for approval before acting.
